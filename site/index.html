From research to outcomes

How we estimate lives saved

We start with deaths in the population a treatment could actually help, then apply realistic treatment reach and the additional mortality reduction compared with current care.

Each input needs evidence from a comparable setting. Overlapping tracks can cover the same patients, so their estimates cannot be added. Queue progress records research work. Changes in patient outcomes require clinical evidence.
