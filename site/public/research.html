These are the questions we’re working on and the evidence behind them. The ranges explore what might be possible if a treatment succeeds and reaches the people who need it.

What it takes to change care
  1. Start with a question. Find out who is missing care and why. Cervical cancer screening and follow-up is our first cancer question. Lung and liver cancer are candidates for future work.
  2. Check the evidence with others. A health service can help test a better way to deliver established care. A new treatment or test may need independent lab work.
  3. Find out what changes for patients. Clinical partners evaluate the approach against current care and look at benefits and harms.
  4. Help proven care reach people. Work out how patients can afford and get it, and measure whether the improvement lasts.
