Funding the research

Help a study take its next step.

If you’re here because someone you love is ill, thank you for taking the time to read this. A gift should come with a clear answer about what it will pay for.

There’s no study asking for donations right now.

We’re testing ideas with computational studies first. If a lab study could answer a question that matters, we’ll explain the work and what it costs before asking for help.

Where your gift would go

You’d give directly to the hospital or university foundation running the study. It would hold the money for that study, pay its bills, and send you the receipt. Remedy Atlas and its organisers would have no access to the money.

What you’d see

Before giving, you’d see who’s doing the research, why it matters, what the budget covers, and what happens if there’s money left over. Afterward, we’d share what the partner spent and what the study found, including when the answer was disappointing.
