## First cancer track and candidates for expansion

- **Cervical cancer screening and follow-up · active.** Where could better access to HPV screening, follow-up, and timely treatment prevent the most deaths? WHO estimates about 280,000 cervical cancer deaths worldwide in 2024. The first project compares implementation gaps and published evidence in one setting. [WHO cervical cancer fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/cervical-cancer/)
- **Lung cancer.** Which gaps in molecular testing and access to biomarker-matched care most affect survival in a defined population? IARC identifies lung cancer as the leading cause of cancer death worldwide in 2024. A first project could focus on one setting and one treatment-eligible subgroup. [IARC 2024 global cancer statistics](https://www.iarc.who.int/news-events/global-cancer-statistics-2024-globocan-estimates-of-incidence-and-mortality-worldwide-for-34-cancers-in-186-countries)
- **Liver cancer.** Where could better surveillance for people already at high risk improve detection and access to effective care? IARC places liver cancer among the leading causes of cancer death worldwide in 2024. A first project could assess where earlier detection leads to treatment and improved survival. [IARC 2024 global cancer statistics](https://www.iarc.who.int/news-events/global-cancer-statistics-2024-globocan-estimates-of-incidence-and-mortality-worldwide-for-34-cancers-in-186-countries)

These are candidates for focused evidence reviews. Their order does not establish a priority or predict impact. Oncology tracks need disease-specific reviewers and a credible route to validation or implementation.

## What earns a place in the queue

Start with a specific population and a consequential, addressable cause of death. Identify what current care misses, the evidence that can change a decision, and who could validate and deliver an improvement. Compare expected reach, effort, and time to patient benefit. Use dated primary sources. Where evidence is missing, name the uncertainty or propose work to resolve it.

Each week the network looks for recent clinical and public-health studies and brings those leads to the recurring portfolio review. Maintainers help decide which findings justify new work. When a slot opens, Workstead starts the highest-priority approved track. Propose an addition by submitting an evidence question through the [issue form](https://github.com/tomoliveri/remedy-atlas/issues/new/choose).
