# Crowdsourced research stays open

[Project home](../README.md) · [Live dashboard](https://remedy-atlas.tomoliveri.workers.dev)

**Anyone can read, reuse, and build on Remedy Atlas findings.** We publish original research summaries and findings we have rights to share under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The project’s code uses the [MIT license](../LICENSE). Share original work you have authority to license. Cite third-party sources and respect their terms. Keep private patient information out of the public repository.

**No company gets exclusive ownership of our findings.** We publish results, uncertainty, and negative findings without a paywall. Funding and research partnerships must preserve the right to publish and share those findings. Donors and partners receive no exclusive rights to them.

**Affordable access is a condition of partnership.** For studies we help advance, we require a written access plan before committing to a development or validation partnership. The plan should state who will be able to get the intervention, how the partner will address affordability, and how it will report progress. Where the project controls results or rights, we preserve public access to the findings and seek non-exclusive routes to development and delivery.

Open findings let other teams verify the work and pursue competing options. The eventual price and availability of a drug or test also depend on development, manufacture, licensing, regulation, and procurement. We publish the commitments we can secure so readers can hold partners to them.
